# What Works in 2026: Ad Creative Formats and Tactics for Consumer Mobile Apps on Meta and TikTok (for Divit)

Research date: 2026-10-10. Source quality legend: **[PLATFORM]** = published by Meta/TikTok/RevenueCat/AppsFlyer (first-party or MMP data); **[VENDOR]** = agency/tool blog (directional, often self-interested); **[CASE/SELF-REPORTED]** = founder or newsletter claims, unaudited. Most 2026 "benchmark" pages found are vendor blogs and they disagree with each other, so numbers below are ranges, not facts.

## 1. Which formats are winning for app installs/subscriptions in 2026?

### Takeaway

The best-documented organic-to-paid format for indie consumer subscription apps in 2025-2026 is the **faceless TikTok/IG photo-mode slideshow** posted at high volume across several accounts, with a visual hook tied to one app feature. UGC-style native video is the paid-ad default on TikTok. Hard 2026 platform data comparing formats head-to-head (slideshow vs. talking-head vs. screen recording) was **not found**. Evidence is mostly case studies and vendor guidance.

### Cited Findings

- **Faceless slideshows (case evidence, self-reported):**
  - Stronger (workout tracker) ran 15 branded accounts (IG @strongermobilereels; TikTok @strongerus, @strongermobile, each with 100M+ total views). Its viral TikTok slideshows were **2 slides**: a selfie-style cover, then an app-feature slide. A faceless 3D body-model hook was reused across posts, with memes and trends layered on top. Reported results: App Store rank went from #432 in early Sept to #14 in Oct, plus 80K downloads/month, $100K MRR and 82M+ views in October. Pricing was $11.99/mo or $69.99/yr with a 7-day trial. Article dated 2025-11-09 and gives no source for the revenue figures. [CASE/SELF-REPORTED] — [Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/100k-mrr-with-faceless-slideshows)
  - Scrolly reportedly posted **6,535 slideshows over about 365 days** across several accounts, for 671M views and about $80K/month. [CASE/SELF-REPORTED, X thread] — [Thread Reader](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1830617819802992989)
  - POV Camera reportedly reached $100K+ MRR and 200K downloads with faceless wedding-themed slideshows on multiple accounts, partly built from repurposed wedding photos. [CASE] — [Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/pov-camera-app-faceless-tiktok-strategy-to-100k-mrr-200k-dl)
  - Fig (food scanner) reportedly passed $100K MRR on an "aggressive slideshow strategy". Health and water scanners reportedly reached 100M+ views. [CASE] — [Social Growth Engineers](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/how-water-and-food-health-scanners-hit-100m-views)
  - AbMax (solo 18-year-old founder) reportedly reached $55-60K MRR and 3,000+ subscribers. He used faceless TikTok/Reels slideshows to test hooks _before_ building features, and added paid ads later. [CASE] — [Stork.ai](https://www.stork.ai/blog/how-one-18-year-old-built-a-60k-app-with-5-tools)
  - Rayz reportedly hit $80K MRR and 40M+ views from faceless TikTok. [CASE, podcast] — [The Edward Show](https://sites.libsyn.com/479997/80000-mrr-with-this-faceless-tiktok-strategy)
  - Feynman AI ("volume over virality": dozens of TikTok accounts with AI-drafted scripts, about $25K/mo) came up only in a search-engine summary, and I could not tie it to a specific fetched page. **Low confidence, re-verify before citing.** [CASE, UNVERIFIED]. A related 0-to-370K-users TikTok + ASO story: [MRRStory](https://www.mrrstory.com/stories/from-0-to-370k-users-using-tiktok-app-store-seo)
  - Couple Joy: a faceless slideshow growth teardown. [CASE] — [Playkit Report](https://playkit.substack.com/p/couple-joy-faceless-slideshow-growth)
  - **Hype flag:** the claim "Cal AI $1.5M MRR in 34 days" circulates on listicles with no primary source. Treat it as unverified.
- **Photo Mode mechanics:** launched in 2022. A post holds up to 35 images, and guides recommend 5-10 slides at 1080x1920. Slideshows must be posted through Photo Mode. A video assembled from images is _not_ swipeable. [VENDOR] — [Multilogin](https://multilogin.com/blog/mobile/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok/), [Shopify](https://shopify.com/blog/how-to-make-a-slideshow-on-tiktok)
- Vendors claim TikTok's algorithm gives swipeable content a "freshness boost". No primary data supports this. [VENDOR, unverified] — [Carouscale](https://www.carouscale.com/en/tiktok-slideshow)
- App-specific slideshow idea lists exist (vendor content). — [Thirds.ai](https://thirds.ai/blog/tiktok-slideshow-ideas-for-app-marketing)
- **Native/UGC-style video for paid:** TikTok-sourced guidance, as relayed by third parties, says ads should look like organic content and use vertical video, UGC-style storytelling, creators and trending formats. [VENDOR relaying PLATFORM] — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026), [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creatives)
- **Storytelling hooks** (narrative arc, emotional twist) took only 6% of spend in Social and Search apps, yet had the **highest Day-7 retention (8.4%)**. Source: AppsFlyer 2025 creative report, 1.1M video creatives, 1,300 apps, $2.4B spend, Q1 2024 to Q1 2025. [PLATFORM/MMP] — [AppsFlyer press release](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/ai-emotion-creative-trends/), [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/creative-variety-becomes-a-strategic-imperative-says-appsflyers-2025-report/)
- Meta estimated **+7% conversions** for businesses using its gen-AI image generation, and **+22% ROAS** for advertisers turning on Advantage+ creative for the first time (Dec 2024, possibly dated). [PLATFORM] — [Meta Engineering](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)

### Inferences

- Divit's "receipt photo, then a tap per person, then an exact amount owed" flow is very visual and fits the 2-5 slide pattern well. Examples: slide 1 is a relatable group-dinner selfie or receipt with a hook ("the friend who 'just had a salad'"); slide 2 is the app screen showing a $14.37 vs $41.20 split. This is the same "selfie cover, then feature slide" structure Stronger reported.
- Slideshows are cheap to make with AI, which suits a solo founder. The case evidence consistently points to _volume across multiple accounts_ rather than polish.
- Talking-head founder content, green-screen, and POV skits are widely recommended, but I found no 2026 quantitative evidence ranking them against slideshows.

### Gaps

- No platform-published 2026 data comparing slideshows, faceless video, UGC, founder-led content, screen recordings, green-screen and POV/memes on install or subscription outcomes.
- None of the case studies are audited. Revenue is self-reported or estimated by tracker tools.
- No bill-splitting or fintech-specific slideshow case study found.

## 2. Paid mechanics: Spark Ads, Smart+, Advantage+ app campaigns, Partnership ads, placements; organic-first then boost

### Takeaway

Both platforms are consolidating into AI-automated single campaign flows: TikTok's unified Smart+ and Meta's unified Advantage+, where legacy Advantage+ App Campaign creation ended in Q1 2026. Boosting proven organic posts still works. On TikTok that means Spark Ads (now with a separate Smart+ workflow). On Meta it means Partnership ads, which Meta says average 19% lower CPA. "Post organically, then put money behind winners" remains the most cited low-budget path, but no platform document explicitly recommends it as the solo-founder default.

### Cited Findings

- **TikTok Smart+ upgrade:** manual and Smart+ flows have been unified into a single flow, with full, partial or no automation. [PLATFORM] — [TikTok Help: Smart+ Upgraded Experience](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-updates-to-smart-plus?lang=en)
- **Smart+ App Campaigns** automate setup and optimization and shift budget toward the best-performing creative. [PLATFORM] — [TikTok Help: About Smart+ App Campaigns](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/about-smart-plus-app-campaigns?lang=en)
  - A third-party source says Smart+ does not allow manual placement selection. [VENDOR] — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/tiktok-smart-campaigns-guide-benefits)
  - Rollout varies by objective, account and market. [VENDOR] — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/tiktok-smart-campaigns-guide-benefits)
- **Spark Ads:** the creation guide (updated June 2026) covers Manual and Search campaigns, with a separate guide for Spark Ads under Smart+. [PLATFORM] — [TikTok Help: Spark Ads creation guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/spark-ads-creation-guide)
  - One vendor claims Spark and non-Spark ads cannot be mixed in a campaign. Unverified. — [Blckalpaca](https://blckalpaca.at/en/knowledge-base/social-media/paid-social-performance-marketing/tiktok-ads-smart-plus-gmv-max)
- **Meta consolidation:**
  - Meta announced on 2025-10-08 that it is deprecating the legacy Advantage+ Shopping and Advantage+ App Campaign APIs. New ASC/AAC campaign creation ends in Q1 2026 in favor of the unified Advantage+ structure. [Trade press reporting a Meta changelog] — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-deprecates-legacy-campaign-apis-for-advantage-structure/)
  - A vendor reports the Feb 2026 unified interface makes AI optimization the default. — [1ClickReport](https://www.1clickreport.com/blog/meta-advantage-plus-campaign-setup-2026)
  - Since Feb 2026, new Sales, Leads and App Promotion campaigns reportedly launch with all Advantage+ Creative enhancements on by default. [VENDOR] — [Confect](https://confect.io/tactics/meta-andromeda-2026)
  - Unverified vendor claim that the Meta app-campaign learning threshold dropped from 50 to 15 conversions/week in April 2026. — [Benly](https://benly.ai/learn/meta-ads/advantage-plus-updates-2026)
  - Meta's page-level ad-count cap is reportedly being relaxed in some accounts. — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/meta-drops-250-ad-page-cap-in-some-accounts-as-286-ads-keep-running/)
- **Partnership ads (Meta):**
  - Meta's own data, via eMarketer, shows **19% lower CPA and 13% higher CTR** on average vs. standard formats. [PLATFORM via trade press] — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/meta-expands-partnership-ads-turn-creator-content-performance)
  - Single-brand cases claim 20-50% lower CPA. [VENDOR/CASE] — [Influee case](https://influee.co/bg/content-hub/babylovegrow-case-study), [Aspire](https://www.aspire.io/blog/partnership-ads-2025-hack-to-slash-paid-ad-costs)
  - Caution: one vendor says most teams see little improvement without operational scaffolding. — [Moburst Intercept](https://intercept.moburst.com/meta-partnership-ads-playbook-to-cut-cpa-by-19/)
- **Placements:**
  - Smart+ automates TikTok placements.
  - On Meta, Advantage+ placements are the default under the unified structure.
  - No 2026 platform data found on Reels vs. Stories vs. Feed CPI for apps.

### Inferences

- Solo-founder path supported by the evidence:
  1. Post organic slideshows and videos.
  2. Identify outliers by views and saves.
  3. Spark-boost the winners on TikTok, or run them as Partnership ads on Meta when made with a creator.
  4. Feed winners into Smart+ / Advantage+ as creative inputs rather than micro-managing targeting.
- Because Partnership ads need a creator handle, a cheap micro-creator collaboration (for example, a college or travel creator) may be worth testing on Meta, given the 19% CPA advantage.

### Gaps

- No official Meta 2026 doc on Advantage+ app campaign creative limits was verified.
- No platform data was found on Reels/Stories/Feed CPI splits for apps.
- Whether Photo Mode slideshows can run as Spark Ads inside Smart+ App campaigns was not confirmed. Check the TikTok Help Center.

## 3. Hooks, first 1-3 seconds, length, captions, sound/music rules

### Takeaway

State the value proposition in the first 3 seconds, and keep the hook inside the first 6 seconds. That guidance is attributed to TikTok, but via third parties. Design for sound-on with native text overlays. **Business accounts and ads are limited to TikTok's Commercial Music Library**, so trending songs generally cannot be used in ads or on business-account posts.

### Cited Findings

- TikTok guidance, as relayed by third parties: introduce the proposition in the first 3 seconds and prioritize the hook in the first 6 seconds. [VENDOR relaying PLATFORM] — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026)
  - The stat behind it varies by source. One says 63% of top ads deliver the main message in the first 3 seconds; another says 67%. [CONFLICTING] — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-creative-best-practices-2026); [Megadigital](https://megadigital.ai/en/blog/tiktok-ad-creative/)
  - "71% decide within 3 seconds" comes from a third party, not TikTok. — same sources
- **Music:**
  - Switching to a Business account locks the in-app sound picker to the Commercial Music Library (CML). — [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/why-can-i-only-use-commercial-sounds-on-tiktok)
  - CML size is reported as 1M+ sounds in TikTok's help center vs. 600K+ on TikTok's business blog. — [Soundstripe](https://www.soundstripe.com/tiktok)
  - Commercial users should not use Original Sounds uploaded by other users. — [Foxi Music](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/commercial-music-licensing-tiktok-guide/)
  - Brands work around this by copying a trend's format and text layout with different, licensed audio. — [Planoly](https://planoly.com/blog/commercially-licensed-vs-trending-sounds-what-can-brands-use)
- **Retention:** AppsFlyer found storytelling hooks retain best at Day 7 (8.4%). [MMP] — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/creative-variety-becomes-a-strategic-imperative-says-appsflyers-2025-report/)

### Inferences

- Divit hook bank aligned with "value in 3 seconds":
  - Show the receipt with "who ordered the $68 wagyu?"
  - "Splitting evenly is a scam when you only had water"
  - "POV: you're the friend who always fronts the bill"
- The music restriction matters for the many-accounts slideshow playbook. Personal (creator) accounts can use trending sounds organically, but those posts may then not be eligible for boosting with that audio. Verify before scaling.

### Gaps

- No official 2026 TikTok or Meta ideal-length figure was found. The often-quoted "21-34 seconds" could not be sourced.
- No data on caption/text-overlay lift specific to 2026.

## 4. Creative volume, testing cadence, fatigue, Andromeda, minimum budgets

### Takeaway

Andromeda (Meta's retrieval engine, Dec 2024) rewards _conceptually distinct_ creatives. Near-duplicates are reportedly treated as one ad. So the goal is more _different concepts_ (different angle, persona, format), not more minor variants. Vendor rules of thumb range from 6 to 30 distinct creatives per ad set. TikTok's practical floor is about $20/day per ad group, but stable learning needs roughly 50 conversions per ad group per week.

### Cited Findings

- **Andromeda (Meta-published):**
  - It narrows tens of millions of ad candidates to thousands.
  - Claimed gains: +6% recall and +8% ads quality on selected segments. In one month, 1M+ advertisers made 15M+ ads with gen-AI tools.
  - Meta plans changes for a "more diverse set of ad candidates". Dated 2024-12-02.
    — [Meta Engineering](https://engineering.fb.com/2024/12/02/production-engineering/meta-andromeda-advantage-automation-next-gen-personalized-ads-retrieval-engine/)
- **Vendor interpretation:**
  - Andromeda looks at conceptual uniqueness, not ad count. Use fewer ad sets with more creatives.
  - Recommended counts conflict: 6 minimum per ad set, 8-12 concepts per campaign, or 20-30 per ad set.
  - A single Five Nine Strategy test (+17% conversions at 16% lower cost) is widely re-quoted.
    [VENDOR] — [Segwise](https://segwise.ai/blog/meta-andromeda-update-creative-strategy-2026), [TheOptimizer](https://theoptimizer.io/blog/how-to-test-ad-creatives-on-meta-after-the-andromeda-update-2026-playbook), [Jetfuel](https://jetfuel.agency/metas-2026-algorithm-update-what-andromeda-changed-and-how-to-adapt-your-ads/), [SparkUGC](https://www.sparkugc.com/resources/meta-andromeda-creative-strategy-2026)
- **Volume at scale (AppsFlyer 2025):**
  - Top 2% of creatives took 43% of non-gaming spend.
  - High-spending non-gaming apps averaged **2,365 creative variations per quarter**, up 18% YoY.
    [MMP] — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/creative-variety-becomes-a-strategic-imperative-says-appsflyers-2025-report/), [AppsFlyer](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/ai-emotion-creative-trends/)
- **TikTok budgets and learning:**
  - The official help page says: during learning, raise budget by no more than 40% per change and wait at least 2 days between changes. [PLATFORM] — [TikTok Help: budgets](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget)
  - $20/day ad-group minimum and $50/day campaign minimum. [VENDOR] — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-minimum-budget-guide)
  - About 50 conversions per ad group per week to exit learning. [VENDOR] — [Stackmatix](https://stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-minimum-budget-guide), [AdManage](https://admanage.ai/blog/tiktok-ads-cost)
  - For installs, budget 20x target CPA, or 10x CPA per day, depending on the source. [CONFLICTING VENDOR]

### Inferences

- With Divit's install-optimized CPI likely at $3-10, 50 installs/week means about $150-500/week per ad group. A trial- or subscribe-optimized event at 50/week is likely unaffordable at first, so start with install or registration optimization and move later.
- For a solo creator, a realistic cadence looks like:
  - **Organic:** 1-3 slideshows per day per account, across 2-4 accounts. This mirrors the case studies but at a smaller scale.
  - **Paid:** 5-10 _conceptually distinct_ ads per week (different angles: "fair split", "trip chaos", "Venmo awkwardness", "tax/tip math"; different formats: slideshow, screen demo, POV skit).

### Gaps

- No platform-published 2026 creative fatigue timeline (days to fatigue) was found.
- No verified official Meta minimum budget for app campaigns was found.

## 5. Benchmarks: CPI, CPM, CTR, install-to-trial/paid for finance/utility apps (2025-2026)

### Takeaway

US iOS finance CPIs are widely reported in the **$3.50-$20** range depending on source and definition. Vendor-sourced general CPI is $2.00-$5.50 on Meta and $1.75-$4.00 on TikTok. TikTok app-install CPM is about $10-13 and TikTok CTR about 0.5-1.8%. RevenueCat 2026 data matters most for Divit's freemium model: **freemium Day-35 trial-to-paid median is 2.1%**, vs. 10.7% for hard paywalls.

### Cited Findings

- **CPI:**
  - Finance iOS US: $3.50-$9.00 (one vendor), $5-14 (another), $8-18 on TikTok iOS (another), $8-20 for neobanks. Some "2026" pages actually cite 2024 AppsFlyer data. [VENDOR, CONFLICTING] — [SEMNexus](https://semnexus.com/cpi-benchmarks-app-category-platform-2026), [Ad-Stack](https://www.ad-stack.ai/blog/cpi-benchmarks-mobile-apps-2026/), [TheViralApp](https://theviralapp.com/blog/tiktok-cpi-benchmarks-app-category-2026/), [IdeaEquity](https://ideaequity.ai/blog/cost-per-install-benchmarks-2026)
  - General CPI: Meta $2.00-5.50 vs. TikTok $1.75-4.00. TikTok is said to suit ages 18-34 and Meta ages 35+ for finance. [VENDOR] — [Insert Affiliate](https://insertaffiliate.com/blog/mobile-app-user-acquisition-cost-benchmarks/), [Apsteq](https://apsteq.com/blog/cpi-benchmarks-2026/)
- **TikTok CPM and CTR:**
  - App-install CPM is about $10-13, with a median of $12.40 across a 3,000+ campaign dataset.
  - CTR is 0.5-1.5% typical and above 2% excellent. Another dataset reports a 1.77% average CTR in 2025 (up from 1.56%) and CPM up about 16% to $13.26.
    [VENDOR] — [Lebesgue](https://lebesgue.io/tiktok-ads/tiktok-ads-benchmarks), [Influee](https://influee.co/gb/blog/tiktok-ads-benchmarks), [TrendTrack](https://www.trendtrack.io/blog-post/tiktok-vs-meta-cpm)
- **RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (published 2026-03-19, 115K+ apps, $16B revenue) [PLATFORM] — [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026):
  - Day-35 trial-to-paid median: hard paywall 10.7% (down from 12.1% in 2025) vs. freemium 2.1%.
  - Revenue per install at Day 60: hard paywall $3.09 vs. freemium $0.38.
  - Trials of 17-32 days convert at a 42.5% median vs. 25.5% for trials under 4 days.
  - 55.4% of 3-day-trial cancellations happen on Day 0.
  - 35% of annual cancellations happen in Month 1, and about 72% of annual subscribers cancel in Year 1 (up from about 56%).
  - AI apps have a higher Year-1 LTV ($30.16 vs. $21.37 median) but 36% worse monthly retention.
- From secondary write-ups of the same report (verify against the original): North America Day-35 download-to-paid median is 2.6%. Download-to-trial is 8.9% for higher-priced apps vs. 4.3% for lower-priced apps. — [RevenueCat SOSA page](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps), [ARPU Brothers](https://arpubrothers.com/blog/revenuecat-subscription-app-report-2026/)

### Inferences

- Rough unit economics for Divit as a freemium app at $34.99/yr: at about $0.38 revenue per install by Day 60, paid CPIs of $3-10 do not pay back quickly. Paid should be a small amplifier of proven organic winners, not the main engine, unless the opt-in 14-day annual trial lifts conversion well above the freemium median.
- The RevenueCat finding that longer trials convert better (17-32 days: 42.5%) supports the owner's 14-day annual trial decision.

### Gaps

- No credible 2026 platform-published CPI/CPM for the finance or utility category on TikTok vs. Meta specifically.
- The Liftoff 2026 report was not retrieved.
- No Meta CPM/CTR benchmark for app installs was found.
- No category-level (finance/utility) install-to-trial figure was found in the RevenueCat summary.

Many indie subscription apps credit multi-account faceless slideshows for $25K-$100K+ MRR. All figures are self-reported or come from growth newsletters that sell courses or tools (Social Growth Engineers runs a community and paid program), so treat them as existence proofs, not expected outcomes.

### Cited Findings

- See Section 1. Summary:
  - Stronger: $100K MRR, 15 accounts, 2-slide format, 82M views/month
  - Scrolly: 6,535 slideshows/year, 671M views, about $80K/mo
  - POV Camera: $100K MRR, 200K downloads
  - Fig: $100K+ MRR
  - AbMax: $55-60K MRR, solo
  - Rayz: $80K MRR
  - Feynman AI: $25K/mo with dozens of accounts
    — [SGE Stronger](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/100k-mrr-with-faceless-slideshows), [Thread](https://threadreaderapp.com/thread/1830617819802992989), [SGE POV](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/pov-camera-app-faceless-tiktok-strategy-to-100k-mrr-200k-dl), [SGE scanners](https://www.socialgrowthengineers.com/how-water-and-food-health-scanners-hit-100m-views), [Stork](https://www.stork.ai/blog/how-one-18-year-old-built-a-60k-app-with-5-tools), [Edward Show](https://sites.libsyn.com/479997/80000-mrr-with-this-faceless-tiktok-strategy), [MRRStory](https://www.mrrstory.com/stories/from-0-to-370k-users-using-tiktok-app-store-seo)

  - Cal AI "$1.5M MRR in 34 days" has no primary source.
  - Most of these apps use hard paywalls with trials (Stronger: 7-day trial, most features locked), which RevenueCat shows convert about 5x better than freemium. Their results may not transfer to a freemium app.
  - Survivorship bias: no data on how many apps ran the same playbook and failed.

### Inferences

- The common elements:
  1. One instantly legible visual hook tied to the core feature.
  2. Many accounts and posts.
  3. Reuse of winning templates with meme or trend variations.
  4. Paid spend only after organic winners emerge.
- Divit's hook asset is the "receipt to who-owes-what" reveal. Its funnel differs from the case-study apps (freemium, social and invite-driven). A plus: each bill shared with friends is itself a distribution loop.
- Multi-account operation may run into TikTok and Instagram rules on inauthentic behavior. Not researched here, so check the platform community guidelines before running many accounts.
