# Competitor Ads and Social Content: Bill-Splitting Apps (as of 2026-10-10)

Method note: I read the Meta Ad Library live in a real Chrome session (logged out) on 2026-10-10, because WebFetch to facebook.com failed with "socket hang up". I also read the TikTok Commercial Content Library (library.tiktok.com) and TikTok discover/video pages. Library URLs are given below so anyone can re-check them. Important limits:

- **Meta, US:** only ACTIVE commercial ads appear. Inactive ads appear only if they ran in the EU/UK in the past year. Ads that ran only in the US and have stopped are invisible, so a Meta "start date" proves only that an ad is running now.
- **Logged-out Meta:** scrolling stopped loading after about 25 to 30 cards per search.
- **TikTok:** the library only covers ads shown in the EU/UK/EEA, so US-only TikTok ads are not visible.
- **Not accessed:** TikTok Creative Center Top Ads (needs a login), Sensor Tower and Appfigures (paywalled).

## 1. What the Meta Ad Library shows for each competitor

### Takeaway

Splitwise is the only incumbent visibly spending on Meta right now. It runs about 19 active **static-image** ads that started between Aug 14 and Oct 9, 2026, and they push **Splitwise Pro discounts** ($59.99 → $41.99/yr, "30% OFF your first year"). Tricount ran a short EU burst (Aug 21 to Sep 17, 2026). The most creative receipt-scan copy came from small AI apps (Call It Even, ScanSplit, Money Buddy) and a Malaysian bank (Ryt Bank). They ran only for days or weeks, which points to testing rather than proven winners.

- Static images dominate split-app ads. Video is underused compared with the finance-app benchmark (Rocket Money, about 2/3 video).

## 4. Angles competitors use, and the Splitwise paywall backlash

### Takeaway

Angles in use: "free" (Tricount), "track who owes who" + Pro discount (Splitwise), the awkwardness of asking ("Split the bill, not the friendship", "awkward 'Bro, how much was it?'"), group trips and holidays, roommates and rent, and receipt-scan speed ("Scan. Split. Done."). The Splitwise free-tier cap (about 3 to 5 expenses/day) produces steady complaints in reviews, and alternative apps use it as SEO bait ("Splitwise alternatives without the daily cap"). No competitor ad I saw attacks Splitwise directly.

### Cited Findings

- Awkwardness / friendship: Call It Even ("Split the bill, not the friendship"), ScanSplit ("no math, no arguments, no stress"), LetsSettle ("without the awkward 'Bro, how much was it?'"), Postbank ("Dieser unangenehme Moment, wenn die Rechnung kommt"). — Meta Ad Library links in section 1.
- Unequal-order unfairness (closest to Divit's itemized + proportional tax/tip value): Ryt Bank ("One person orders iced water. One person adds squid. Somehow everyone pays the same.") and Call It Even ("who got the guac?"). — section 1 links.
- Trips: Tricount ("best group holidays"), Money Buddy ("group trips without the group-chat math"), Call It Even ("Every group trip has a ghost"), Xpense Flow (Christmas trip). Roommates: LetsSettle, Splitwise ("split the rent"), SplitHome, PaidUp.
- Splitwise backlash: reviews from Dec 2023 / Jan 2024 complain of a 3 to 4 expenses/day free cap introduced "with no communication", with pricing cited as £3.99/month and about $5/month. An aggregator of App Store reviews through Jul 2025 reports growing complaints about "greed" and paywalled features. — [Kimola Google Play report](https://kimola.com/reports/splitwise-app-feedback-report-uncover-user-insights-google-play-en-144452); [Kimola App Store report](https://kimola.com/reports/explore-why-splitwise-users-are-drifting-away-get-insights-now-app-store-in-155789)
- A competitor blog (Finny, biased source) says Splitwise free is capped at about 3 to 5 expenses/day, and that receipt scanning, itemization, charts and currency conversion are Pro-only. — [Finny blog](https://getfinny.app/blog/best-splitwise-alternatives-2026)
- Splitwise's own current ads confirm it is pushing Pro with "30% OFF your first year" / $41.99/yr. — [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&search_type=page&view_all_page_id=194000797330861)

### Inferences

- "Receipt scanning is free here, but Pro on Splitwise" is an attack angle nobody runs in paid ads.
- Apple's iOS 27 feature takes over the plain "scan and split one dinner" pitch for US iPhone users who all have Apple Cash. Divit's messaging should lean into what Apple does not do: Venmo (which most US friend groups actually use), Android and web friends with no app required, multi-receipt trips/events, and running balances across bills.

### Gaps

- I found no Reddit thread data with view or upvote counts from this session. The backlash evidence comes from review aggregators.

## 5. What nobody is covering (white space for Divit)

### Takeaway

No competitor runs **video demos of an actual receipt scan → items assigned → Venmo request** on Meta. None run **creator-led restaurant-table content** at scale, apart from one viral non-brand example. None attack **Splitwise's paywall**, and none position against **Apple's Apple-Cash-only split**.

### Cited Findings (evidence for the gaps)

- All of Splitwise's 19 active ads are static lifestyle images with no scan demo. — [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=active&ad_type=all&country=ALL&search_type=page&view_all_page_id=194000797330861)
- Tricount ads say "free" and "group holidays", with no AI or receipts. — [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?active_status=all&ad_type=all&country=ALL&q=tricount&search_type=keyword_unordered)
- The AI-app ads with scan copy ran for days and mostly linked to Instagram profiles or Play Store, not iOS installs at scale. — section 1.
- Splitwise has no TikTok account, and no split-app ads are visible in the TikTok EU library. — section 2.
- The one viral format (958K likes / about 9.1M views) is creator-at-restaurant, and no brand appears to be scaling it. — [TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/@brandonbee03/video/7653261365762624798)
- Apple's split requires Apple Cash for everyone, and Apple Cash is US-only. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/phones/iphones/ios-27-will-reportedly-offer-bill-splitting-in-apple-wallet-heres-how-it-will-work)

### Inferences (candidate angles Divit could own)

1. **Real-receipt speed demo** (9:16 video, 7 to 15s): a long restaurant receipt → AI fills items → tap faces → each person's total with tax/tip → Venmo requests sent before the server returns. Nobody shows this in paid ads.
2. **"Iced water vs. squid" fairness**: itemized plus proportional tax/tip, so the light orderer stops subsidizing the heavy one. Only Ryt Bank (Malaysia) uses this, and nobody does in the US.
3. **Anti-paywall**: "Scanning receipts shouldn't cost $5/month" / "No 3-expense daily limit". Splitwise is actively advertising Pro, so its users are being reminded of the cap.
4. **Venmo-native, works for the friend with Android / no app**: share link, no download. This counters both Apple (Apple Cash-only) and Splitwise.
5. **Group trip / bachelorette / multi-receipt events**: one trip, many receipts, one settle-up. Tricount owns "holidays" in the EU only, and the US is open.
6. **Creator-at-restaurant UGC program**: copy the brandonbee03 format (real restaurant tag, friend group, app reveal) with micro-creators. Brand-account skits get single-digit likes, so creators beat brand accounts.
7. **Tag-a-friend hooks** ("tag the friend who still owes you") were tested by Call It Even (zodiac version). It is cheap engagement bait but unproven.

### Gaps

- I have no performance data (CTR, CPI) for any competitor creative. "Winner" judgments rest only on run length, and US run length is invisible for stopped ads.
