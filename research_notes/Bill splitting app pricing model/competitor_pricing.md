# Competitor Pricing & Monetization: Bill-Splitting / Expense-Sharing Apps (as of Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-10. App Store prices are US storefront as fetched on that date unless noted. Many third-party "pricing" blogs are written by competing splitter apps (Splitty, Are We Even, HippoSplit, Finny, etc.) and conflict with each other; primary sources (App Store listings, official help centers) are preferred and flagged where only secondary sources exist.

## Splitwise: free-tier limits, Pro price/features, 2023-2026 changes, reception

### Takeaway

Splitwise is a freemium subscription: the free tier is deliberately throttled (help center: "up to 4 expenses each day", plus ads; a 10-second cooldown was reported at rollout in late 2023), and Pro (monthly or annual, ~$4.99/mo; annual US App Store price point $39.99, some blogs say $49.99) unlocks unlimited expenses, receipt scanning/itemization, currency conversion, charts, search. In 2025-2026 Splitwise added a **Duo plan** and an annual **Trip Pass** (one trip group gets Pro for 30 days) bundled with yearly plans — effectively a per-trip unlock mechanism layered on a subscription. The late-2023 limit rollout drew sustained user backlash and is the main reason competitors market themselves as "Splitwise alternatives".

### Cited Findings

- Official help center: Pro plans are **Individual + Trip Pass (annual)**, **Duo + Trip Pass (annual, adds a second person)**, and **Monthly (one person)**. Prices not listed on the page (directs to splitwise.com/pro / Account tab). — [Splitwise KB: What is Splitwise Pro?](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- Official Pro features: no cap on expenses, transaction import from linked card (select countries), expense search, currency conversion, receipt scanning and item-level splitting, spending charts by category, default splits. — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- **Trip Pass**: yearly plans include one annual Trip Pass applied to a chosen trip group, giving all group members Pro features for 30 days. — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- Official free limit: free users can add "up to 4 expenses each day"; resets next day; workaround suggested is to have a friend add the expense. — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro); quoted also by [Splitty (competitor blog)](https://splittyapp.com/learn/splitwise-free-limits/)
- US App Store IAP list (all named "Splitwise Pro", no period labels): $2.99 (x3), $3.99, $4.99 (x4), $29.99, $39.99. Rating 4.0 from ~29K ratings; latest version 26.9.1 (Sep 20). Listing also mentions 10GB receipt storage, OCR receipt scanning & itemizing, JSON backups as Pro. — [App Store: Splitwise](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433)
  - Inference on mapping: $4.99 = monthly, $39.99 = individual annual, $29.99 / $2.99 / $3.99 likely regional/legacy/promo or intro tiers — unconfirmed.
- Annual price conflict: $39.99/yr per App Store & HerMoney (2025); $49.99/yr per Are We Even (2026). — [Split-Circle blog](https://split-circle.com/en/blog/splitwise-free-vs-pro); [Are We Even](https://www.areweeven.com/blog/splitwise-free-vs-pro-2026); [Splitty roundup](https://splittyapp.com/learn/best-bill-splitting-apps/)
- History: 2021 TechCrunch reported Pro at **$3/month** and that Splitwise takes no cut of payments (so subscriptions are the revenue model). Splitwise raised a $20M Series A led by Insight Partners in April 2021. — [Crowdfund Insider](https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2021/04/174712-interpersonal-finance-app-splitwise-raises-20-million-through-series-a-funding-round-led-by-insight-partners/) (TechCrunch $3/mo claim via search summary of [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2145011))
- Late 2023 rollout: users reported a sudden daily cap (most said 3/day, some 4) and a **10-second cooldown** between adds on Android and iOS; frustration voiced on X. — [ITVoice](https://www.itvoice.in/splitwise-has-introduced-restrictions-on-the-number-of-free-expenses-users-can-add); Trustpilot reviews dated Dec 2023 describe no notice. — [Trustpilot AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com)
- Reception persisted into 2025: Trustpilot reviewer (July 2025) switched to Tricount; reviewers call free tier "unusable" for occasional users. Google Play review analysis headlined "Splitwise App Faces Criticism for Changes in Pricing and Limitations". — [Kimola report](https://kimola.com/reports/splitwise-app-feedback-report-uncover-user-insights-google-play-en-144452); [Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com?page=2)
- Plates by Splitwise (dinner-bill itemizer): free, up to 10 people, manual item entry (no scanning), iOS only. — [plates.splitwise.com](https://plates.splitwise.com/)

### Inferences

- Splitwise's model is "throttle the free tier until habitual users convert" — a cap on core usage (expense count) rather than gating only premium features. It monetizes but generates lasting goodwill damage and a whole category of "free Splitwise alternative" marketing.
- The Trip Pass shows Splitwise itself recognizes that trips are episodic and that group-level, time-boxed unlocks are a natural fit — directly relevant to Divit's event/trip model.
- AI/receipt itemization is a Pro-only feature at Splitwise, so Divit offering some free scans is a differentiator.

### Gaps

- Exact current US mapping of each IAP price to plan (Duo price unknown; splitwise.com/pro not fetched).
- Whether the 10-second cooldown is still in force in 2026 (only 2023 reports found).
- Exact date limit changed from 3 to 4/day (if it did) — not found.

## Other competitors: Tricount, Settle Up, Splid, Tab, Splitty, Plates, Spliit, AI-first entrants

### Takeaway

The field splits into (a) fully free (Tricount after bunq; Plates; Spliit), (b) cheap one-time unlocks (Splid $2.99-$3.99; Tab Pro $2.99 reported), (c) cheap subscriptions with **lifetime** alternatives (Splitty $9.99/yr or $24.99 lifetime), (d) **per-group / per-trip time passes** (Settle Up: Group Premium 1 week $5.99 → lifetime $149.99), and (e) **consumable AI-scan credits** (BillSplit: 3 free, packs $1.99-$24.99). Receipt/AI scanning is the most commonly gated feature among newer entrants.

- Settle Up's group-time-pass ladder is the closest existing analog to a Divit "event/trip pass" and validates price points around $6/week to $15/month per group.

## Consumable credits, per-trip/event passes, lifetime purchases — and how AI scanning is gated

### Takeaway

All three exist in the market: per-trip/group passes (Settle Up Group Premium 1wk-1yr + lifetime; Splitwise Trip Pass bundled with annual), lifetime (Splitty $24.99, Settle Up Lifetime Group $149.99), and consumable credits (BillSplit scan credits). AI scanning specifically is gated by subscription (Splitwise), free-quota-then-paid (Splitty: 3 free), or credits (BillSplit: 3 free credits).

### Cited Findings

- Splitwise Trip Pass (30 days Pro for a whole trip group, bundled with yearly plans). — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro)
- Settle Up per-group time passes and lifetime group premium. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985)
- Splitty 3 free scans, $9.99/yr or $24.99 lifetime. — [Splitty](https://splittyapp.com/learn/splitwise-free-limits/)
- BillSplit credits (3 free; packs $1.99-$24.99). — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/billsplit-ai-receipt-scanner/id6746359329)

### Inferences

- A per-event pass maps naturally onto Divit's events, and has precedent at two price levels (Settle Up retail passes; Splitwise as annual-plan perk). Group-level unlock means one payer upgrades the whole group — reduces friction for non-paying friends.
- Because AI scanning has a real marginal cost (LLM inference), credits or capped free scans align cost with revenue better than unlimited free scanning.

### Gaps

- No data found on conversion rates of per-trip passes vs subscriptions.

## Payment-platform competition (Venmo, Apple Cash, Cash App, PayPal)

### Takeaway

Venmo (Groups, Nov 2023) and Apple Cash (Split the Bill with AI receipt scanning via Apple Intelligence, US-only) now offer group tracking and even itemized receipt splitting inside the payment app, with no fee mentioned for the feature. This commoditizes basic splitting and receipt scanning, capping willingness to pay for those alone.

### Cited Findings

- Venmo Groups (rolled out from Nov 14, 2023): track, split, manage, settle ongoing group expenses; any member can add expenses; targets roommates, families, clubs, trips; TechCrunch: it "could potentially cannibalize" apps like Splitwise. No pricing mentioned. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/14/venmo-gets-a-new-way-to-split-expenses-among-groups-like-clubs-teams-trip-buddies-and-more)
- Venmo Groups reported limits: up to 30 members per group, up to 20 groups per user. — [Payments Journal (via search summary)](https://www.paymentsjournal.com/venmo-launches-venmo-groups-to-split-common-expenses); Fox coverage quoted Venmo calling it "one of our most requested features". — [Fox LA](https://www.foxla.com/news/venmo-groups-splitwise-alternative-bill-calculator)
- Apple Cash Split the Bill: photograph/import a receipt, review scanned items, add tip, split by item or evenly, send request in Messages; requests open up to 30 days; receipt scanning requires Apple Intelligence + Siri; Apple Cash is US-only, 18+. No fee mentioned. — [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/127565)

### Inferences

- Divit's Venmo hand-off and AI itemization overlap with free platform features; paid value must come from cross-platform (Android/web, non-Apple-Cash users), multi-receipt trip ledgers, and no-download shareable links — things Apple Cash (iOS-only, US-only) and Venmo Groups (no itemized receipt scanning reported) don't fully cover.

### Gaps

- Cash App and PayPal split features: no reliable source found.
- Whether Venmo Groups added receipt scanning since 2023: not found.

## Public revenue/download signals

### Takeaway

Signals are sparse and inconsistent; Splitwise's revenue estimates range from ~$10K+/mo iOS IAP (AppGoblin) to $1-10M/yr total (Levels.fyi, Lazyweb $3.2M 2024). No reliable estimates found for smaller competitors.

### Cited Findings

- AppGoblin: Splitwise iOS ~341K MAU, ~$10K+/month revenue, 100% IAP / 0% ads (methodology unstated; likely underestimates). — [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/458023433)
- Lazyweb: Splitwise revenue (2024) $3.2M, 2.4M active users (source unstated). — [Lazyweb](https://www.lazyweb.com/company/splitwise)
- Levels.fyi: estimated revenue $1M-$10M, 31 employees. — [Levels.fyi](https://levels.fyi/companies/splitwise)
- Ratings as popularity proxies (US App Store): Splitwise 4.0 / 29K; Splid 4.9 / 4.1K; Settle Up 4.8 / 1.8K; BillSplit (new) too few to show. — App Store listings above.

### Inferences

- Even the category leader appears to earn single-digit millions/year on subscriptions despite a large user base — consumer willingness to pay in this category is low; aggressive free-tier throttling was Splitwise's lever to lift it.
- Splitwise's lower rating (4.0) vs Splid/Settle Up (4.8-4.9) is consistent with the backlash to throttling, though not proven causal.

## Per-app audit (structured blocks, verified 2026-10-10 unless dated otherwise)

### Takeaway
Across the six audited apps, the paywall almost never sits in onboarding. It shows up **on a limit** (Splitwise: daily expense cap; Splid: second group; Splitty: 4th split; BillSplit: 4th scan) or **on a feature tap** (Settle Up: receipt photos, categories, stats). Only Splitwise requires an account up front. Settle Up and Splitwise both sell **group-level unlocks** (Group Premium; Trip Pass). Tricount is entirely free and pays for itself by bringing users into bunq. Free trials (time-limited) were not documented for any of these apps; "N free uses" quotas are the norm.

### Cited Findings

#### 1. Splitwise (Splitwise Inc., US; $20M Series A 2021)
- **Model / prices:** Freemium subscription. US App Store IAP price points are $2.99, $3.99, $4.99, $29.99 and $39.99, all labelled "Splitwise Pro" with no billing period. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433). Third-party blogs give $4.99/mo and $49.99/yr ([Are We Even, Mar 2026](https://www.areweeven.com/blog/splitwise-free-vs-pro-2026)) or $29.99–$39.99/yr depending on region ([ShareCost, Jul 2026](https://budgetbakers.com/cs/products/sharecost/vs-splitwise/)). Plans are Individual + Trip Pass (annual), Duo + Trip Pass (annual, 2 people) and Monthly. — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro). Pricing "varies by country" per the App Store listing.
- **Free-vs-paid gating:** Free gets unlimited groups, equal/percent/exact splits and debt simplification ([Splitty blog](https://splittyapp.com/learn/splitwise-free-limits/)). Pro-gated: unlimited expenses (free is capped at "up to 4 expenses each day"), receipt scanning and item-level splitting, currency conversion, category charts, expense search, default splits, card transaction import (select countries), 10GB receipt storage, JSON backup and no ads. — [Splitwise KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433). Free users see banner ads and interstitials. — [Are We Even](https://www.areweeven.com/blog/splitwise-free-vs-pro-2026)
- **Limit history:** Reported without notice in about Nov/Dec 2023 as 3 (some users saw 4) expenses per day plus a **10-second cooldown** per add. — [ITVoice](https://www.itvoice.in/splitwise-has-introduced-restrictions-on-the-number-of-free-expenses-users-can-add); [Trustpilot](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com). The help center now says **4/day**. — [KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro). The cooldown is still reported in a current App Store review and in a 2026 competitor page. — [App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433); [Splitty](https://splittyapp.com/learn/splitwise-vs-splid-vs-settleup/). Are We Even (Mar 2026) still says 3/day. Inference: the cap was loosened from 3 to 4 at some point; the date is unknown.
- **Receipt scanning:** OCR scanning plus itemization exists and is **Pro-only**. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433)
- **Onboarding:** An account is required (a competitor claim, consistent with the product). — [Splitty](https://splittyapp.com/learn/splitwise-vs-splid-vs-settleup/); [KittySplit](https://www.kittysplit.com/es/splitwise-alternative). Screen count not found. The first paywall appears **on hitting the daily limit** ([Are We Even](https://www.areweeven.com/blog/splitwise-free-vs-pro-2026)) and on Pro-feature tap. One App Store reviewer reports an upsell **while setting up a new trip** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433)). No trial found.
- **Who pays:** Per user, with Duo covering 2 people. The Trip Pass (annual plans only) gives **every member of one chosen trip group Pro for 30 days**, once per year. — [KB](https://kb.splitwise.com/pro/what-is-splitwise-pro). Invited members are free users, so they are subject to the cap, ads and paywall. Inference: the "friend can add it for you" workaround in the help center shows the cap is per adder.
- **Store signals:** iOS 4.0★ from about 29K ratings. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splitwise/id458023433). Revenue estimates conflict: about $10K+/mo iOS IAP ([AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/458023433)), $3.2M in 2024 ([Lazyweb](https://www.lazyweb.com/company/splitwise)) and $1–10M ([Levels.fyi](https://levels.fyi/companies/splitwise)).
- **Pricing changes and reception:** About $3/mo Pro in 2021 (TechCrunch, via [search summary](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2145011)), rising to about $4.99/mo plus the daily cap and cooldown from late 2023. The change drew strong backlash ("unusable", switching to Tricount) that continued into 2025. — [Kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/splitwise-app-feedback-report-uncover-user-insights-google-play-en-144452); [Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com?page=2)

#### 2. Tricount (owned by bunq, a Dutch neobank; EU-centric)
- **Model / prices:** **Completely free.** Premium (about $10/yr, which covered statistics, PDF/CSV export and ad removal; the free version had ads) was **discontinued** with the new app, rolled out on iOS July 1, 2024. — [Tricount help](https://help.tricount.com/d/61452); [tricount.com/new-app](https://tricount.com/new-app); [search summary of Tricount blog](https://tricount.com/en-us/blog/with-the-new-tricount-app-tracking-group-expenses-is-now-easier-than-ever)
- **Gating:** Nothing is paid. Advertised features are unlimited daily expenses, multi-currency with conversion and up to 3 file attachments per transaction. Premium features were **removed rather than paywalled**: CSV/PDF export, personal mode, statistics and custom saved splits. — [Tricount help](https://help.tricount.com/d/61452)
- **How bunq monetizes it:** Bank acquisition. Tricount pushes a **free bunq Credit Card** (prepaid) that can be linked to a tricount so card payments are added as expenses automatically, plus bunq-account linking. Sign-up for that requires a phone number and a tax ID. Former Premium users got 6 months of bunq Pro (€59.94 value). — [Tricount help: Free Credit Card](https://help.tricount.com/articles/who-can-sign-up-for-a-free-credit-card-and-how); [Add bunq payments](https://help.tricount.com/articles/add-bunq-payments-as-expenses); [Tricount blog](https://blog.tricount.com/en/automatically-add-expenses-by-card)
- **Receipt scanning:** No AI scanning or itemization found; only attachments (up to 3 files).
- **Onboarding:** Members can **join a tricount by link on iOS, Android or web without an account** (third-party sources). — [European Alternatives](https://european-alternatives.eu/product/tricount); [PilotPlans](https://www.pilotplans.com/blog/tricount-review). There is no paywall; the upsell is the bunq card/account. Screen count not found.
- **Who pays:** Nobody. The cost is the bunq funnel.
- **Store signals:** US App Store listing not retrievable (404). Not found.
- **Reception:** It is the destination of choice for some users leaving Splitwise ([Trustpilot July 2025](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/splitwise.com?page=2)). Backlash to losing Premium features was not researched.

Group Premium is sold per group, and an App Store reviewer says it is a one-time cost covering all transactions in the group, which can be reset after a trip. Whether every member gets the Premium features was **not confirmed** by an official source.
- **Store signals:** 4.8★ from 1.8K ratings (US).

#### 4. Splid (Splid ApS, Denmark)
- **Model / prices (US App Store):** One-time IAPs, **Splid Plus $3.99** and **2 Groups $2.99**. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/splid-split-group-bills/id991473495). 2019 prices were $1.99 (2 groups) and $2.99 (unlimited) ([2019 blog via search](https://wanderlustphotosblog.com/2019/06/20/new-travel-app-makes-tracking-shared-travel-costs-a-breeze/)). A competitor says each user pays individually to unlock more groups. — [KittySplit](https://www.kittysplit.com/en/splid-alternative)

### Inferences — gaps Divit could exploit
- **Group/event pass priced per trip, bought by one organizer, covering every member.** Splitwise only gives this away once a year inside an annual plan. Settle Up sells it but the member coverage is unclear and it has no AI. A clear "Trip Pass" for one event (around $4.99–$9.99, under Settle Up's $14.99/month group price) with AI scans for the whole group fits Divit's events model.
- **Never cap core logging.** Splitwise's 4/day cap and cooldown are the most-hated pattern in the category and push users to Tricount and Splid. Divit should keep manual entry, people, balances and settle-up unlimited and free, and gate only what has marginal cost (AI scans) or power features.
- **Free AI quota, not a time trial.** Peers use "3 free" (Splitty, BillSplit). Divit could give a few more (for example, a monthly free allowance) to stand out from Splitwise, which gates scanning entirely.
- **Invitees never see a paywall.** Divit's shareable no-download links compare well with Splitwise (account plus cap for every member). Splitty, Settle Up's web view and Tricount show that recipients without the app are the norm among the favourites.
- **Cross-platform AI itemization.** Apple Cash's itemized split is iOS/US/Apple-Intelligence-only. Splitty and Tab are iOS-only. Venmo Groups has no reported receipt scanning. Divit on Android and web plus AI plus multi-receipt events has little direct competition.
- **Offer a lifetime option.** Splitty ($24.99) and Settle Up ($149.99 per group) show that a lifetime purchase sells to subscription-averse users in this low-WTP category, where the leader earns only single-digit millions.
